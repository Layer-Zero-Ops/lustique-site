# Your website setup — a quick guide 🤍
*From Layer Zero Studios, for Zandeline · about 30 minutes, just once*

Hi Zandeline! We're building your new Lustique Co. website, and to get started I need you to set up a few accounts — **in your own name, so you always own everything.** Your part is the easy bit: **create the account, then "invite us in,"** and we do all the technical building. You never touch the technical side.

**The email to invite us everywhere:** **hello@layerzerostudios.com** — and where it asks for a role, choose **Administrator** (or **Editor** for Sanity).

## 1 · Cloudflare — where your website lives  *(free · ~5 min)*
1. Go to **dash.cloudflare.com** → **Sign up** with your email → confirm the email they send you.
2. Top-right, click your account name → **Manage Account → Members → Invite Member**.
3. Type **hello@layerzerostudios.com**, choose **Administrator**, and send.

✅ Done — we'll add your website and handle everything else from here.

## 2 · Brevo — sends your customers' confirmation emails  *(free · ~6 min)*
1. Go to **brevo.com** → **Sign up free** → confirm your email.
2. Click into **Settings → "SMTP & API" → API Keys → Generate a new API key**, and name it **"Layer Zero."**
3. **Send us that key using the secure link at the bottom of this guide** — not by email or WhatsApp.

✅ Done.

## 3 · Google Workspace — your branded `info@` email  *(~$6/month · ~10 min)*
*This puts your `info@lustiqueco.co.za` email onto Google. We'll move your existing emails across together so nothing is ever lost.*
1. Go to **workspace.google.com** → **Get started** → enter your domain (**lustiqueco.co.za**) → complete signup (this one asks for a card — it's yours, about $6/month).
2. Once it's set up, **just reply to let me know** — we'll do the email move together on a quick **20-minute call** (you stay logged in as the owner; I guide you step by step).

✅ Done.

## 4 · Sanity — where you'll edit your own site later  *(free · ~3 min)*
*Your "Lustique Co" project already exists — this just lets us set up the easy editing for you.*
1. Go to **sanity.io/manage** → open the **Lustique Co** project → **Members → Invite**.
2. Type **hello@layerzerostudios.com**, choose role **Editor**, and send.

✅ Done.

## 5 · Google Analytics — visitor stats  *(free · later)*
Nothing to do now — we'll set this up together a little later. Just so you know it's coming. 🙂
